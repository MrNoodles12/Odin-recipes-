Description

This is a delicious pizza recipie that is easy to follow and will take between 30-45 minutes

Ingredients

  • mince
  • onion
  • tomatoes
  • red wine
  • salt, pepper and seasonings of choice
  • dough

Steps

  1. heat oil in frying pan over medium heat and add mince
  2. Add onion and tomatoes
  3. Season accordingly
  4. add red wine
  5. add to dough and put in oven
